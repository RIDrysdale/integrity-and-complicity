# 03 — Literature and access

## Summary

1. No empirical social science, historical testimony or literature is assigned, and no decision records any of these absences. Evidence, high.
2. The free-text rule explains the empirical and contemporary gaps. It admits 15 of 18 mapped classical and religious works and 0 of 20 empirical ones, and the slate has that shape. It does not explain the literary gap, since half the literature passes. Evidence, medium.
3. Practice is stricter than principle 9's "prefer". D-004 and READINGS turn it into a ratchet, and Weber, Havel and Ghazali were excluded despite having no substitute. Judgment, medium.
4. Free principle 8 candidates exist for Modules 4 to 7: *Shabbat* 54b–55a, Mencius 5B1 with *Analects* 18.1, *Zhuangzi* ch. 4, and Bhishma Parva §43. Ghazali Book 14 is in print in English. None has been passage-tested. Judgment, low to medium.
5. Free inquiry reports (TRC vol. 4, Francis 2015, Horizon) could supply the missing historical cases.

## Question

What is the best material on the problem of acting inside a compromised system, across philosophy, the social sciences, history, literature and memoir, and traditions outside the West? What can a reader actually obtain?

## First principles

Written from `00-problem.md` and `METHOD.md` alone, before any program file was opened.

### What the reader needs from literature

The reader in the problem statement is inside, dependent, implicated, benefiting, uncertain and not leaving. Material earns a place if it does one of five things for that person: (1) gives them concepts for their own share of the wrong (complicity, many hands, structural responsibility); (2) shows the mechanisms by which decent people drift, obey and fall silent, so they can see it in themselves; (3) shows real people in the position, including those who stayed and were wrong and those who left and were wrong; (4) lets them rehearse the position in imagination (literature); (5) offers a framing other than the modern Western "individual conscience against the institution" one, for instance the minister's duty of remonstrance, graded response to wrong, or role-based duties.

Two consequences follow before any title is named. First, category (2) is empirical, and nearly all of it was published after 1960 in journals or in copyright books. Second, the texts best suited to category (5) that are also free are classical ones; the modern non-Western work is mostly in copyright.

### Key

Access: **F** free and legal online; **F?** free online but the legality or durability of the copy is uncertain; **L** journal article or scholarly book, normally reached through a library or database; **C** in copyright, bought or borrowed.
Locators: **S** edition-independent and fine-grained (numbered sections or paragraphs, Stephanus or Bekker numbers, chapter and verse, book.chapter); **Ch** edition-independent but coarse (chapters, parts, acts, section headings); **P** page numbers only (edition-bound).
**(w)** marks a bibliographic or availability fact I checked by web search on 2026-09-27. Everything else is from memory. **unverified** marks a detail I am not sure of.

## Findings

1. **The program contains no empirical social science, historical testimony or literature, and no decision records any of these absences.** A grep of the repository outside `review/` for *psycholog*, *sociolog*, *empiric*, *experiment*, *research*, Milgram, Browning, Vaughan, Bandura, Jackall and Hirschman found none in program text, and no DECISIONS entry concerns genre. *Evidence, high.*

2. **The availability rule explains the empirical gap and most of the gap in contemporary philosophy.** None of the 20 empirical works in the map passes the rule, and 2 of 18 philosophical works do; the slate matches what the rule admits. *Evidence, medium* (the counts are my coding of a map built largely from memory). That the rule, not a choice about content, is the cause is *Judgment, medium.*

3. **In practice the rule is stricter than its text.** Design principle 9 says "prefer" free texts and allows in-copyright work that has no adequate substitute. D-004 ("later modules are expected to do better") and READINGS line 59 ("Later modules should do better, not worse") turn this into a ratchet. D-009, D-020 and D-021 each exclude a text for which no substitute exists (Weber, Havel, Ghazali). *Evidence, high,* for the texts. That this is drift, not a considered tightening, is *Judgment, medium*: no entry records a tightening.

4. **Literature is out by choice, not because of access.** Nine of the 18 literary works in the map are free and chapter-locatable, among them *The Warden*, "Ward No. 6", *Ivan Ilyich*, *An Enemy of the People* and *Antigone*. None has been considered. *Evidence, medium.* The more likely cause is the module form, which is built around texts that argue. *Judgment, low*; chunk 04 is better placed to test it.

5. **Public-inquiry reports are free, current, paragraph-numbered sources for the empirical and historical gap.** Web search confirmed three are free online: TRC *Report* vol. 4 (1998), which covers the institutional hearings on business, faith, law, health and media (justice.gov.za); Francis, *Freedom to Speak Up* (2015), at the National Archives; and the Horizon Inquiry *Final Report* vol. 1 (2025). *Evidence, medium.* I verified availability but not paragraph numbering.

6. **Module 3 leaves out the standard contemporary works on its own question** (Kutz 2000; Lepora and Goodin 2013), and no reason is recorded. *Evidence, high,* for the absence. *Judgment, medium*: this is a trade-off, not a plain error. A taxonomy would press on the module's open question ("whether the three readings are answering one question"), but it also risks handing the reader the program's answer.

7. **A free, complete passage has the structure D-021 wanted.** D-021 blocked the Islamic duty because "a single hadith is not a reading". Babylonian Talmud *Shabbat* 54b–55a scales responsibility to one's capacity to protest, from household to town to world (w). At 55a, the Attribute of Justice refuses "it would not have helped" because the righteous could not have known that (w). It is free on Sefaria, cited by folio, and bears on Module 4. *Evidence, medium* (search snippets only). Whether it counts for principle 8 depends on whether the Talmud is outside the Western canon. READINGS implies it is not, and the question is Robert's.

8. **The remonstrance material READINGS reserves for Modules 4 and 6 is free and on point.** Mencius 5B1 names four sages by how they served bad rulers or refused to: Bo Yi "pure", Yi Yin "responsible", Liuxia Hui "accommodating" and Confucius "timely" (w, secondary wording). *Analects* 18.1 counts three ministers as *ren*: one who left, one who feigned madness, and one who remonstrated and died. Module 6 treats the options as "rival uses of the same occasion". These texts may contest that by indexing the right response to the person and the time. *Judgment, low to medium*; it must pass the passage test that D-010 failed.

9. ***Zhuangzi* ch. 4 aims most directly at "an individual agent deciding"** (ARCHITECTURE line 89). Confucius dissuades Yan Hui from going to reform a tyrant with a plan, prescribes the "fasting of the heart", and Yan Hui ends by saying there is "no more Hui" (w, modern translation). Legge's 1891 translation is free. *Judgment, low*: readings of the passage vary, and the project has withdrawn claims of this kind before.

10. **Two candidates bear on Module 7's dependence question.** In *Mahabharata*, Bhishma Parva §43 (Ganguli, free), Bhishma says "a man is the slave of wealth… I have been bound by the Kauravas with wealth" (w). The section directly follows the *Gita* sections of the same parva (w), so it could meet the open question on the *Gita* by being paired with it. Ghazali's *Iḥyāʾ* Book 14 has a complete English translation (DeLorenzo, Islamic Texts Society, 2014, w). It covers stipends from rulers and relations with rulers and their officials (w). It is in copyright and in print. *Evidence, medium,* for the bibliography; *Judgment, medium,* for the fit.

11. **Gandhi's *Hind Swaraj* (1909) may restore the claim that D-020 records as lost**, that going along constitutes the system. It has numbered chapters and is freely hosted, but its licence is **unverified**. *Judgment, low.*
