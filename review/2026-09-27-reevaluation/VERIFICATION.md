# Verification of SYNTHESIS.md

Checked against reports 00–04 and the repository (`review/METHOD.md` §4). No other file was edited.

## Summary

**Verdict: usable with the listed corrections.** The repository facts are right, and the sources that could be reached support the bibliographic claims. The faults are in how the synthesis reports the chunks:

- **Wrong citations.** D-023 in convergence 1, 02-F2 in convergence 2, 01-F9 and 01-R1 in convergence 4. Conflict D cites 04, which never mentions literature.
- **Judgments promoted.** "Excluded by the sourcing rule" is the main one. 03 tested its own strict rule, not principle 9, which says "prefer" and admits in-copyright work.
- **Convergence 3 is not a convergence.** It is the orchestrator's inference, and "arguably the central experience" rests on no finding. Recommendations 7, 9 and 10 are stronger than their sources.
- **Dropped findings,** including 01-F7 (relief deferred), 04-F10 and 03's "classical texts suit this reader".

## Corrections required

1. **Convergence 1: "Three reports converge … 01-F1, 01-F2, 04-R5 and D-023."**
   - D-023 is about the decision log, CLAUDE.md and CHECKLIST. It says nothing on readers or outcomes.
   - 04 makes no finding about outcomes.

   *Fix:* "01-F2 and 04-R5 find no reader test before completion. 01-F1 alone finds no stated outcome." Drop D-023, and soften "strongest convergence".

2. **Verdict: the reader picture, and "Most findings are consequences of it".**
   - 01-F1 and 01-F2 show only that nothing has been tested. The picture itself comes from 01-F3 (Judgment, medium–high), 02-F3 and 02-F4.
   - Many findings do not follow from it: 04-F1 to F3, 04-F7, 03-F3, 02-F2 and 02-F5.

   *Fix:* cite those findings, and write "several findings, in the orchestrator's judgment".

3. **Convergence 2.**
   - (a) "No decision records … (01-F3, 02-F2, 03-F1)". 02-F2 is about E3, not collective action. ARCHITECTURE l.89 does record that the carving assumes "an individual agent deciding".
   - (b) "Every reading is a text that argues" is from 03-F4 (Judgment, low), not 03-F1.
   - (c) 01-F3 and 01-F4 are Judgments, but are stated as plain findings. 01-F4's "though less than it seems" is dropped.
   - (d) Calling 02-F3 and 03-F1 "the same narrowing", and "inherited rather than chosen", are the orchestrator's reading.

   *Fix:* correct the citations, add the confidence labels, and mark (d) as the orchestrator's judgment.

4. **Convergence 3: "excluded by the sourcing rule" and "None of the 20 … passes the free-text rule".**
   - 03's test was its own strict "free and not page-located" coding.
   - DESIGN-PRINCIPLES 9 says "prefer" and admits in-copyright work where there is no substitute.
   - "Sourcing rules" is a different WORKFLOW section.
   - 03-F2 is Evidence, medium, from a map built largely from memory. The claim of causation is Judgment, medium.

   *Fix:* "none would pass a strict free-and-locatable test (03-F2); practice has tightened toward it (03-F3)."

5. **Convergence 3: "noticing … has no home at all".** 02 says Arendt "partly touches" noticing, and lists the module bodies as not checked. *Fix:* "no designated home (02-F4)".

6. **Convergence 3: "not visible from any single chunk", "reinforce each other's blind spot", "arguably the central experience of the charter's reader".**
   - No finding supports these claims. 02-F4 (Judgment, medium) says only that drift is met before deciding.
   - "Most people enter by increments" is from 02's first-principles section, not a finding.
   - CHARTER l.7 lists drift as one of four examples.

   *Fix:* retitle the section "Orchestrator's inference", and label the drift claim as untested judgment or delete it. Recommendations 9 and 10 should cite 02-R3 and 03-R1, not "convergence 3".

7. **Convergence 4: 01-F9 as "the cheapest available measure"; "One page would serve both purposes (04-R2, 01-R1)".**
   - 01-F9 says the built-in comparison measures change, not improvement, on a case the reader has already seen. That is why 01-R1 uses an unseen case.
   - 01-R1 is the pilot, not the positions page.

   *Fix:* cite 04-R2 alone for the page, and add 01-F9's caveat.

8. **Conflict C and recommendation 7: "Reports 01 and 04 both recommend a pilot of Module 1".** 04-R5 asks for a timed test of Module 1 *or 2* on *one* outside reader. *Fix:* offer it as a cheaper option beside 01-R1's five to eight readers.

9. **Conflict D: "04 would expect them to strain a form built around argument".** 04 does not mention literature. The point is 03-R5 and 03-F4 (Judgment, low), and no chunks conflict here. *Fix:* attribute it to 03, and present it as a decision, not a conflict.

10. **Conflict B: "option (b) … adds the least load".**
    - Option (c), merging Modules 4 and 6, adds no net module.
    - 02's warning that (b) "risks being flagged again rather than filled" is dropped.

11. **Conflict A: "would readmit … Havel and Weber (03-R2)".**

12. **Recommendation 17: the inquiry reports supply people "who stayed and were right".** 03 (c15) says Horizon volume 1 covers impact and redress, and that volumes on insiders are unverified. *Fix:* "may supply; not checked."
